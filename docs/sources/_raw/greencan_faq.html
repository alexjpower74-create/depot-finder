FAQ's
Commonly asked questions asked by clients.

1.Where is your main office located?

2.How will I know when my cheque is ready?

3.If milk cartons are recyclable, why aren't they accepted?

4.Can my Account be used right away?

1. WHERE IS YOUR MAIN OFFICE LOCATED?
Our main office is located on 79 Blackmarsh Road, across the street from Purity Factories - same building as our Blackmarsh Road Depot.
2. HOW WILL I KNOW WHEN MY CHEQUE IS READY?
When requesting cheques online from your Recycling Account, your cheque will be ready after 5-10 business days. If you requested to pick up your cheque, you will know it is ready when your account balance is updated.
3. IF MILK CARTONS ARE RECYCLABLE, WHY AREN'T THEY ACCEPTED?
Ever Green only deals with containers that consumers pay a deposit on at the time of purchase. Considering milk cartons do not require a deposit, Ever Green does not accept them.
4. CAN MY ACCOUNT BE USED RIGHT AWAY?
Yes. When you set up a Green Depot Account, you can use it right away. You can then upgrade your Green Depot Account to an Ever Green Recycling Account with a username and password. Once registration is complete, your account is activated immediately and ready to use.
