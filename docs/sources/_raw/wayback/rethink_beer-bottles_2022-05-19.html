Beer Bottles

There are different programs for different types of beer bottles.

Imported beer bottles and beer cans – are accepted through MMSB’s Used Beverage Container Recycling program and can be returned to a Green Depot for refund.

Refillable beer bottles – are not part of the Used Beverage Container Recycling Program. Local beer brewers such as Labatt, Molson and Quidi Vidi operate an independent deposit-refund system. You can return these refillable bottles to beer retailers or to a Brewers Bottle Depot to receive a refund.

Please note: Some Green Depot locations accept refillable beer bottles as an added service for their customers. Please call your local Green Depot to confirm whether refillable beer bottles are accepted as well as the amount of the refund.
