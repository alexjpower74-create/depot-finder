    WASTE MANAGEMENT

    Garbage Collection Schedule

    The Town of Bonavista provides garbage collection from residential dwellings that are within our municipal boundaries. Pick up and disposal of domestic garbage is completed on a regular weekly basis. This schedule may change because of holidays, inclement weather, breakdowns and other unforeseen circumstances. However, every effort will be made to revert back to this schedule as soon as possible.

    Bonavista Waste Management Site

    The Municipal Landfill site is located on Route 235 about 4 km outside of Bonavista.

    ​

    If you have any questions regarding the Municipal Management Site please contact the Public Works Department.

    ​
