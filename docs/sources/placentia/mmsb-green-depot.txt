Placentia Green Depot (Dunville)
Placentia Green Depot (Dunville)
Main Highway (Across from Hickey’s Nursery)
 Placentia, NL
Placentia Green Depot is part of a province-wide network of Green Depots accepting used beverage containers included in MMSB’s Beverage Container Recycling Program.
NOTICE – January 29, 2026
We are pleased to announce the reopening of the Dunville Green Depot on Wednesday, February 4, 2026 at 8:30 a.m.
As households may have been holding onto extra materials during the closure, we appreciate your patience as we work through the initial backlog. For the first two weeks following reopening, customers are asked to limit drop-offs to a maximum of 10 bags. Please note that bulk or blitz drop-offs will not be accepted during this period.
