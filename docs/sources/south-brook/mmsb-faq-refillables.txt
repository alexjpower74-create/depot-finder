Why do some Green Depots accept refillable beer bottles, but others don’t?
 Refillable beer bottles are not part of MMSB’s Used Beverage Container Recycling Program because local beer brewers such as Labatt, Molson, and Quidi Vidi operate an independent deposit-refund system. 
As an added service for their customers, some Green Depot locations will accept refillable beer bottles. Please call your local depot to confirm whether refillable beer bottles are accepted as well as the amount of the refund. Find your Green Depot location here. 
Refillable bottles can be returned to some beer retailers or to bottle depots to receive a refund.
Note: ALL imported beer bottles and beer cans purchased in the province are accepted through MMSB’s program and can be returned to a Green Depot for refund.

 Why do some Green Depots accept additional materials (such as paper and electronics), but others don’t?
 The Green Depot network is comprised of a network of independently owned and operated businesses. Some locations offer services that are separate from the used beverage container licensing agreement with MMSB, meaning services offered will vary from location to location.
