Stephenville Green Depot
Stephenville Green Depot
196 Hansen Memorial Highway
 Stephenville, NL
Stephenville Green Depot, owned and operated by Scotia Recycling Limited Newfoundland, is part of a province-wide network of Green Depots accepting used beverage containers included in MMSB’s Beverage Container Recycling Program. This depot also accepts paint and electronics. Scotia Recycling will also accept limited amounts of paper and cardboard from residential customers.
